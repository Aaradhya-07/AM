## Competitive interpretation

- There is no empty capability category.
- No single public competitor found clearly spans the complete proposed lifecycle.
- A combination of Spec Kit/BMAD, BackArch/ArchGenie, model/hardware tools, a coding agent, CodeBoarding/NodeScope, PromptScan, and AWS Transform can approximate most of it.
- ANVILMARK therefore needs a proprietary decision model and conformance loop. Aggregation and UI polish alone will not be defensible.
