Anvilmark adapts the dataset's latest published Parquet splits into a
versioned SQLite snapshot. It preserves leaderboard configuration, category,
published date, score, confidence interval, sample count, and source metadata.
The presentation and schema have been changed. Arena has not endorsed this
adaptation.
